## Agent S eval: Nemotron Super vs local qwen3:4b
Same 4 golden streams (2 real, 2 hand-built) and 5 questions.

| | Progress in range | Status right | Uncited claims dropped | Q&A good | Failed calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen3:4b (local, dev fallback) | 4/4 | 4/4 | 1–2 per run | 4/5 to 5/5 | 0 |
| **Nemotron Super**, before retries | 3/4 to 4/4 | 3/4 to 4/4 | **0** | 4/5 to 5/5 | 1 per run in 2 of 5 runs (timeouts) |
| **Nemotron Super**, with retries | **4/4** ×3 | **4/4** ×3 | **0** | **5/5** ×3 | **0** |

**What the numbers say:**
- **Every miss was a timeout, never a wrong answer.** Every stream Nemotron answered was in range with its status right. The misses were requests where Token Factory stalled ("The read operation timed out").
- **The fix:** `agent_s/llm.py` retries once on a timeout, a 429 or 5xx error, or malformed JSON. Ultra then falls back to Super (PLAN.md §10.1). The eval now counts failed calls separately from wrong answers.
- **Grounding:** Nemotron never wrote an uncited item. The 4B model needed 1–2 dropped per run.
- **The live miss is fixed.** The local model answered "Is anything blocked?" with "no evidence" in the 13 Oct live run; Nemotron answered it right every time.
- **One prompt change** (a worked "not yet" example) took Nemotron's Q&A from 4/5 to 5/5, and moved qwen3:4b from 5/5 to 4/5. We keep it: Nemotron is the production model.
